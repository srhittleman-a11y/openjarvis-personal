OpenJarvis (personal)

This is a private, single-user setup of the open-source OpenJarvis assistant, running on its owner's own computer. It is not a public service and is not offered to anyone else.

Privacy Policy

Who uses it: only the owner of this Google Cloud project, signed in with their own Google account.

What Google data it accesses: with the owner's permission, it reads Gmail, Google Calendar, Google Tasks, Google Contacts and Google Drive. It may archive or label emails and accept or decline calendar invitations, only when the owner approves.

Where data goes: Google data is processed locally on the owner's computer by a local AI model. It is not sold, shared, or sent to any third party, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Storage: sign-in tokens and cached data are stored only on the owner's computer and can be deleted at any time. Access can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google API Services User Data Policy: this app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Contact: the support email listed on the app's Google sign-in screen.
